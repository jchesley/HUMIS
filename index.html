Birds of Prey

About Birds

Birds are warm-blooded animals that have feathers, wings, and beaks. Most birds can fly, and all birds lay eggs.

Learn more about birds on Wikipedia .

What Are Birds of Prey?

Birds of prey are birds that hunt and eat other animals. They have sharp claws called talons, strong beaks, and excellent eyesight.

Some common birds of prey include eagles, falcons, and owls.

Learn more about birds of prey

1. Bald Eagle

The bald eagle is a bird of prey found in North America. It is known for its white head, brown body, and yellow beak. It is also the national bird of the United States.

Three Interesting Facts

  • It mainly eats fish.
  • It builds very large nests.
  • It lives near lakes and rivers.

Bald Eagle - Wikipedia

2. Peregrine Falcon

The peregrine falcon is one of the fastest animals in the world. It can reach speeds over 200 miles per hour when diving to catch prey. It is found on nearly every continent.

Three Interesting Facts

  • It hunts other birds in flight.
  • It can live in cities and on cliffs.
  • It has excellent eyesight.

Peregrine Falcon - Wikipedia

3. Great Horned Owl

The great horned owl is a large owl found throughout the Americas. It gets its name from the feather tufts on its head that look like horns. It is a powerful nighttime hunter.

Three Interesting Facts

  • It mainly hunts at night.
  • It eats rodents, rabbits, and birds.
  • It can fly almost silently.

Great Horned Owl - Wikipedia

Conclusion

Birds of prey are important to nature because they help control populations of other animals. Each species has unique abilities that help it survive.
